Good to know

Frequently asked.

Is MCUSCS affiliated with Marvel or Disney?

No. MCUSCS is an independent fan research project and is not endorsed by or affiliated with Marvel or Disney.

Is the archive complete?

No. It is under construction. The public previews show the organization currently being built. A live coverage list should be provided before purchases begin.

What does lifetime access mean?

The final purchase terms must specify the lifetime scope, how updates are handled, and what happens if the Discord server or product is discontinued.

Can I preview the server before buying?

Yes — view the screenshots on this page. A public Discord preview invite can be added once its link and preview permissions are configured.

Are the calculations always conclusive?

No. Calculations depend on assumptions, and arguments should distinguish direct observations from interpretations and uncertainty.
